# 0.1.33 (local preview)

- Fix missing large Pokédex portraits with National Dex and G9 Battle Sprites.
  The index and entry pages use the installed provider's animated images.
  Its Dex and animation switches remain supported.
- Restore the real Gen 2 wallet balance in both Bag skins, including the
  item-PC deposit chooser. Improve the key pocket icon at small sizes.
- Retain 0.1.32's original organiser artwork and distribution checks.

For G9 portraits, use game 0.2.61, install the companion's artwork, and keep
G9 Battle Sprites → DEX SPRITES enabled. Select BATTLE ART or DEFAULT for the
suite's Menu Sprites; an explicit CRYSTAL selection keeps that provider.
Gen 1 ADVANCED colours and Gen 2 GBC colours preserve the artwork's RGB values;
authentic limited-palette modes retain the game's colour remapping.
